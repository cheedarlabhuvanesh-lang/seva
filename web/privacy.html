Privacy

What Seva keeps, and what it never asks

Seva has no accounts. It never asks for your name, your phone number, your Aadhaar or any document. There is nothing to log in to, because there is no account to make.

What Seva never does

Never asks for your location. Seva does not know which hospital or station you are in.
Never keeps a recording of the hall. Audio is read for its numbers and dropped.
Never saves what other people said. Only your own number reaches your history.
No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
Nothing is sold or shared. There is no company behind this and no one to sell it to.

On this phone right now

This is the whole of what Seva has stored, read live from this phone as you look at it. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

When a number is announced

The phone listens, quietly A loudness check runs on the phone itself. Silence never leaves the device.
Only the loud part is sent When someone is actually announcing, that short clip goes to Gnani's speech service to be turned into text, then is gone.
The text is matched, not stored Fixed rules look for your number in that text. The text itself is thrown away. Only the result is kept.
Your phone speaks The alert sentence goes to Gnani's voice service and comes back as sound.

As it stands today this app makes no network call at all — everything above happens once the laptop is connected. You can check: there is not one live request in the page source.

When you ask a question

Your question text is sent to the laptop, which adds the hospital's own written information and asks a language model to put it in plain words.

The model only rewrites. It is never given a recording, never given an announcement transcript, and is not used as a source of facts. If the answer is not in the hospital's list, Seva says so instead of inventing one.

The key that reaches those services lives on the laptop and is never put in this page.
